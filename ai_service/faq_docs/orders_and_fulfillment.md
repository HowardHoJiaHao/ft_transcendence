# Seller Orders and Fulfilment

When a buyer checks out, you get a real order — not only a chat message. This guide is for sellers using **My Shop** (`/profile/my-ads`).

RacketBot can explain this process. It cannot mark orders delivered, change stock, or operate your dashboard for you.

## 1. Where orders appear

* Open **My Shop** from the header (**My Shop**).
* Pending buyer orders are loaded from the seller orders list.
* You also receive a notification when someone places an order or when you later mark it delivered (if notifications are working on your session).

Buyers see the same order on **Cart → My Purchases**.

## 2. What a pending order means

A pending order means:

* The buyer committed to that listing and quantity at the listed price.
* Stock was reserved or reduced according to the checkout rules.
* You still need to hand over or post the item.
* For **Meet up / COD**, payment is usually collected by you in cash or DuitNow.
* For **Stripe sandbox**, payment confirmation is handled by Stripe webhooks. Only fulfil when the purchase shows as paid if you are using that demo path.

Read the quantity, listing title, and buyer identity before you travel or post.

## 3. Preparing the item

* Confirm you still have that exact frame or unit (especially if you listed more than one).
* Pack mailing orders in a tube or hard case. Photograph the packed item and the tracking slip.
* For meet-ups, bring the racket, any extras you promised (bag, overgrip), and a way to check DuitNow.

## 4. Mark as delivered

* When the buyer has the item, use **mark as delivered** on that order in My Shop.
* That sets the order to **completed / delivered**.
* If that quantity finishes the listing, the listing can move to a sold state so it no longer appears as available.
* The buyer can then leave a listing review and optionally fill the recommendation survey.

Only mark delivered after handover or confirmed delivery. Marking early creates a review window for an item the buyer does not have.

## 5. Cancelling

* If you cannot fulfil (broken frame, already sold outside the cart, buyer no-show after a genuine attempt), use the seller **cancel** action on that order when it is offered.
* Agree the cancellation in chat first so the buyer is not waiting at a hall.
* After cancel, check that stock looks right. If the item is gone for another reason, update or remove the listing.

## 6. Stock and listing status

* `stockQuantity` is the number of units buyers can still order.
* Checkout reduces available stock. Do not keep advertising “plenty left” in the description if stock is 1.
* A listing with pending or sold state may be locked against edits.
* If you sold the item only in chat, without a cart order, the site will not know. Edit stock or take the listing down yourself.

## 7. After delivery

* Reply to the review if the product page allows it; stay factual.
* If the buyer reports a crack that was disclosed, point to the listing photos and chat.
* If the buyer reports a crack that you hid, that can lead to a staff suspension.

## 8. What sellers cannot expect the platform to do

* It will not collect meet-up cash for you.
* It will not automatically book a courier.
* It is not obliged to mediate every dispute or issue a refund.
* Staff can suspend accounts that sell clones as genuine or ignore paid/recorded orders, but that is a staff action.
