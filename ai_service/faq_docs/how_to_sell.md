# How to Sell Badminton Gear on RacketDiction

Selling on RacketDiction is free. You list the item, buyers find it through search or RacketBot, and they either chat with you or place an order from the cart. You remain responsible for the description, photos, stock, and handover.

## 1. Before you list

* Sign in. Selling is only available on an active account.
* Confirm you own the item (or are authorised to sell it) and that it is legal to sell.
* Do not list counterfeit, stolen, or deliberately misleading gear.
* Have clear photos ready: overall shot, frame, cone/serial area, grip, and any damage.

## 2. Create a single listing

* Open **Sell** / **Post an Ad** from the header or shop area.
* Write an accurate title that a buyer would search for, for example `Yonex Astrox 88D Pro - 4U G5`.
* Choose the correct **category**:
  * Rackets
  * Grips
  * Shuttlecocks
  * Racket Bags & Covers
  * Strings
  * Shoes
  * Apparel
  * Accessories
  * Others
* If the category is **Rackets**, set:
  * **Weight:** 1U through 8U
  * **Shaft flex:** Stiff Shaft, Medium Flex, or High Flexibility
  * **Balance:** Head Heavy, Even Balance, or Head Light
* Set **condition:** New, Second-hand (Used), or Refurbished.
* Set **stock quantity** (at least 1). This is how many units buyers can order.
* Enter **location** (city or area) and **dealing method:** Meet Up, Mailing or Delivery, or both.
* Optionally add a contact phone number shown on the listing.
* Write a description that mentions string brand/tension, paint chips, cracks, grip condition, and why you are selling.

Wrong category or spec labels make the listing hard to find. Filters and RacketBot search use the exact values above.

## 3. Pricing

* Enter the asking price in RM. It must be greater than 0.
* You may add an **original price** to show a struck-through “was” amount. That is optional and is not verified.
* Say in the description whether you will negotiate. Checkout uses the listed price unless you change the listing first.

## 4. Photos

* Upload up to **6** clear photos per listing.
* Include close-ups of:
  * The racket head and grommets (cracks, chips)
  * The cone and any serial marking
  * The grip and shaft
  * The item next to a common object if scale is unclear
* Do not reuse another seller’s catalogue photos and claim they are your item.

## 5. After the listing is live

* It appears in search and on your **My Shop** page (`/profile/my-ads`).
* Buyers may message you through **Chat Seller**. Check messages often.
* Buyers may also add the listing to their cart and check out. That creates a real order.
* Keep stock up to date. If you sell the last unit, the listing can leave the active marketplace once the order is fulfilled.

## 6. Managing listings in My Shop

* Open **My Shop** to edit title, price, description, stock, and photos on listings that are still editable.
* Listings in some states (for example pending or sold) cannot be freely edited.
* Use **Bulk upload** / **Import** if you have many similar items. See the CSV import guide.
* Do not leave a sold-out listing active at stock 0 if you no longer want enquiries.

## 7. Closing a deal

* If the buyer only chatted and will pay at a meet-up without using the cart, agree the place, time, and payment, then update or remove the listing so other buyers are not misled.
* If the buyer checked out, fulfil the order from My Shop: confirm the item, hand it over or post it, then **mark the order as delivered**.
* After delivery is marked, the buyer can review the listing.

For pending orders, cancellations, and stock behaviour, see the seller fulfilment guide.

## 8. Seller responsibilities

* Honour the price and stock you published.
* Describe condition honestly. Hidden cracks and clone rackets sold as genuine are not allowed.
* For mailing, agree who pays postage and send a real tracking number.
* For meet-ups, choose a public badminton hall, MRT/LRT station, or mall.
