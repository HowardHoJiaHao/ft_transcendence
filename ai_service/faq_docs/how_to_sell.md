# How to Sell Badminton Gear on RacketDiction

Turn your unused badminton rackets, bags, and shoes into cash! Selling on RacketDiction is completely free.

## 1. Creating a Listing
* Log into your account and click the **Sell** or **Post Ad** button.
* Enter a catchy and accurate title (e.g., "Yonex Astrox 88D Pro - 4U5 G5").
* Select the correct racket categories:
  * **Weight:** 3U, 4U, 5U, etc.
  * **Shaft Flex:** Stiff, Medium Flex, High Flexibility.
  * **Balance:** Head Heavy, Even Balance, Head Light.
* Write a detailed description: Mention string type, tension (e.g., 26 lbs), paint chips, cracks, and the reason for selling.

## 2. Setting Your Price
* Enter your price in Malaysian Ringgit (RM).
* Specify if the price is fixed or negotiable.
* Set an optional "Original Price" to show buyers the discount they are receiving.

## 3. High-Quality Photos
* Upload up to 6 clear, well-lit photos.
* Ensure you take close-ups of:
  * The racket head (to check paint chips or cracks).
  * The cone and serial number (for authentication).
  * The grip condition.

## 4. Closing the Deal
* Check your messages frequently. Buyers will message you through the built-in RacketDiction chat system.
* Agree on the dealing method: **Meet Up** at a public court/MRT station or secure **Mailing/Delivery**.
* Once sold, mark the listing status as **sold** in your profile dashboard to avoid further inquiries.
