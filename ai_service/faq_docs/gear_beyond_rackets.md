# Shoes, Strings, Grips, Bags and Other Gear

The racket spec guide covers frames. Most RacketDiction listings are not frames. This page is practical buying language for the other categories on the site: Shoes, Strings, Grips, Shuttlecocks, Racket Bags & Covers, Apparel, Accessories, and Others.

None of this replaces a shop fitting. It is here so you know which questions to ask in **Chat Seller** and which photos to demand.

## 1. Court shoes

Indoor badminton shoes are not running shoes. The gum or non-marking rubber and the wide forefoot matter more than a brand logo.

* **Last / width.** Many Asian court shoes come in 2E or 3E. A narrow street shoe on a sweaty hall floor is how ankles roll.
* **Gum rubber vs herringbone running outsoles.** Ask the seller whether the pair was used only indoors. Outdoor concrete kills indoor gum rubber in a few sessions.
* **Cushioning vs stability.** Heavier or knee-sensitive players often want more heel cushion. Fast doubles players often prefer a lower, flatter shoe.
* **Used pairs.** Check the pivot point under the ball of the foot, the inner heel counter, and whether the upper has already blown out at the flex crease.
* **Size.** Court shoes can feel small. Ask for the labelled US/UK/cm size and an insole measurement photo.

If a listing is under Shoes but the photos are dusty outdoor trainers, treat them as street shoes, not hall shoes.

## 2. Strings

String is a consumable. A used racket’s string is part of the frame’s story, not a free upgrade.

Common club patterns (names you will see in Malaysian listings):

* **Durable / control-ish workhorses** such as BG65 or BG65Ti — last longer, a bit muted, popular with beginners and rental frames.
* **Thinner repulsion** strings (many 0.66–0.68 mm club favourites) — more bite and sound, break sooner, need more consistent tension.
* **Hard / control** strings that advanced players pair with high tension.

Tension, again in brief:

* **18–23 lbs** — larger trampoline, easier depth, less control. Typical for new players or fragile frames.
* **24–27 lbs** — common club range.
* **28+ lbs** — control for strong technique; more stress on the frame. Do not crank a tired second-hand frame to 30 lbs because an Astrox 99 video did it.

Always ask: **which string**, **when it was strung**, and **whether the grommets are already cut**. A “just restrung” claim should come with a photo of the fresh tie-off.

## 3. Grips

* **Towel grips** absorb sweat and die fast in Malaysian halls. Ask how many sessions they have.
* **Synthetic PU grips** last longer and feel tackier when new.
* **Overgrips** are cheap consumables. A dirty overgrip is not a defect; a mouldy or unravelled replacement-grip on the wood handle can be.
* Check whether the original wooden handle is swollen or cracked under the grip if the listing is a used frame.

Grips as a standalone category should list pack size and whether they are original or generic.

## 4. Shuttlecocks

* **Feather vs nylon.** Feather is standard for club games; nylon lasts for training and windy outdoor use.
* **Speed / grade.** Malaysian halls often use mid speeds; mountain or air-con halls can need a different speed. Ask the seller which speed number is printed on the tube.
* **Condition.** Opened tubes dry out. Ask how many shuttles remain and whether the corks are still round.
* Beware listings that photograph a premium tube and ship a mixed used dozen.

## 5. Bags and covers

* Count the racket slots (3, 6, 9) and whether there is a ventilated shoe pocket. Never put damp shoes in the same pocket as feathers or a spare frame if you can avoid it.
* Used bags: check zips, the shoulder-strap stitch, and mould in the shoe compartment.
* A simple thermal cover is not the same as a 9-racket pro bag. The category name on the listing should match the photos.

## 6. Apparel and accessories

* Apparel: state size (S–XXL or a cm chart). Club jerseys shrink. Ask for a flat-garment photo with a tape.
* Accessories: ankle braces, dampeners, wristbands, stringing tools. Say whether they are used against skin (hygiene) or only in a bag.

## 7. How to use filters and chat

* Home-page weight / flex / balance filters apply to **racket** listings. They will not sort shoes by width.
* For non-racket gear, search by brand, size, or category, then confirm the details in chat.
* Ask for a photo of the label (shoe size, string model, shuttle speed) every time the title is vague.
