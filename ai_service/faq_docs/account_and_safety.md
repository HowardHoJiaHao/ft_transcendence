# Account Settings, Security & Platform Safety

Your account security is our top priority. Learn how to secure your RacketDiction profile and avoid common scams.

## 1. Account Setup & 2FA
* **Registration:** Anyone can sign up for a free RacketDiction account using a valid email address and password.
* **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):** Enhance your login security by enabling 2FA. Go to your **Profile Settings** and turn on the 2FA option. This protects your account from unauthorized logins by requiring a verification code.
* **Email Verification:** Complete email verification when the account flow asks for it. Verification is one account signal, not a guarantee that a transaction is safe.

## 2. Recognizing & Avoiding Scams
* **Too Good to Be True:** Be wary of high-end rackets (like Yonex Astrox 99 Pro or Voltric Z-Force II) priced unusually low (e.g., RM100 instead of RM800). These are likely fake (clone) rackets.
* **Advanced Payments:** Never transfer deposits to a seller before seeing the racket, especially if they claim "other buyers are offering more."
* **External Communication:** Keep all negotiation and messages within the built-in RacketDiction chat system. Do not switch to external apps immediately.

## 3. Reporting a Seller or Listing
* If you encounter a seller attempting to sell clone/fake rackets as genuine, or acting suspiciously, report them immediately.
* Keep screenshots of the listing, seller profile, chat, and payment evidence.
* Contact platform support with that evidence. RacketDiction does not currently expose a complete in-app report workflow, so do not rely on a Report button being available.
