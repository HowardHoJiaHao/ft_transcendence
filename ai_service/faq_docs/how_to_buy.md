# How to Buy Badminton Gear on RacketDiction

Welcome to RacketDiction! Finding and purchasing the perfect badminton equipment is super easy. Here's a step-by-step buyer's guide:

## 1. Finding Equipment
* Browse rackets, strings, shoes, and bags using our **Search Bar** or filters.
* You can filter by weight, flex, balance category, and location.
* Click on any listing card to see the full details, seller rating, and pricing.

## 2. Reviewing the Listing Detail
* Check the racket specs! Look at the weight (e.g., 3U, 4U), flexibility (e.g., Stiff, Medium, Flexible), and balance (e.g., Head Heavy, Head Light, Even).
* Look at the price (listed in RM). Ensure it fits your budget.
* Review the item condition. We support both brand new and used (second-hand) equipment.

## 3. Contacting the Seller
* RacketDiction is a user-to-user marketplace.
* To buy or negotiate, click the **Chat Seller** button on the product details page.
* You must have a free registered account to message sellers.
* Chat with the seller to negotiate the price, clarify racket specs, and agree on the dealing method.

## 4. Purchasing
* Decide on a dealing method with the seller: **Meet Up** (cash or instant transfer on delivery) or **Mailing/Delivery** (mailing courier).
* Verify seller feedback ratings. Try to deal with highly rated, verified sellers for absolute safety.
