# How to Buy Badminton Gear on RacketDiction

RacketDiction is a Malaysian marketplace for badminton equipment. You can browse without an account. To message a seller, add items to the cart, or place an order, you need a free registered account.

There are two complementary buying paths:

* **Negotiate first:** open the listing and use **Chat Seller** to ask questions, request extra photos, or arrange a meet-up inspection.
* **Order through the cart:** add the listing to your cart and check out with **Meet up / COD** or **Stripe sandbox card payment**.

Those paths can be used together. Many buyers chat first, then check out once they are happy with the condition and price.

## 1. Finding equipment

* Use the search bar in the header for titles, brands, models, or seller names.
* On the home page, filter racket listings by **weight** (1U–8U), **shaft flex**, and **balance**.
* Categories you will see include Rackets, Grips, Shuttlecocks, Racket Bags & Covers, Strings, Shoes, Apparel, Accessories, and Others.
* Click any listing card to open the product page.

RacketBot can also search live listings for you if you describe the gear, budget, and specs you want.

## 2. Reading a listing carefully

Before you pay or travel to a meet-up, check:

* **Price** in Malaysian Ringgit (RM). An optional original (struck-through) price is only a seller claim.
* **Condition:** New, Second-hand (Used), or Refurbished.
* **Stock quantity.** You cannot buy more than the listed stock.
* **Location** and **dealing method** (Meet Up, Mailing or Delivery, or both).
* **Racket specs** when the category is Rackets: weight (for example 4U), shaft flex, and balance.
* **Photos.** Look for cracks, paint chips, the cone, grip wear, and the serial area.
* **Listing reviews** on that product. A listing rating is about that listing only. It is not a full seller reputation score.

Listing titles and descriptions are written by the seller. Treat them as claims until you inspect the item or see extra proof.

## 3. Contacting the seller

* Open the product page and click **Chat Seller**.
* You must be signed in.
* Use chat to confirm remaining stock, string tension, cracks, postage cost, and where to meet.
* Stay on RacketDiction chat for the negotiation. Do not move to WhatsApp or Telegram just because someone asks you to pay first.

Chat does not by itself create an order. If you want the purchase recorded in **My Purchases**, use the cart.

## 4. Adding to cart and checking out

* On the product page, choose a quantity within the available stock and add it to the cart.
* Open **Cart** from the header. You must be signed in to check out.
* Choose a payment method:
  * **Meet up / COD** — creates pending orders immediately. You still arrange handover with the seller.
  * **Stripe sandbox card payment** — test-mode card checkout. No real money is charged. Cards only, and every item must belong to the configured demo seller.
* After a successful meet-up checkout, the cart clears and **My Purchases** opens.
* After Stripe checkout, you are sent to Stripe and then back to the site. The purchase appears after the payment webhook is confirmed.

For the full checkout, order-status, review, and survey steps, see the checkout guide.

## 5. After you buy

* Open **Cart → My Purchases** to see pending, delivered, cancelled, or refunded orders.
* For meet-up orders, the seller’s phone may appear so you can coordinate handover.
* When the seller marks the order delivered, you can leave a listing review and optionally fill the recommendation survey. The survey helps RacketBot’s racket-spec suggestions; it is not required to keep the item.

## 6. What buying on RacketDiction does not include

* The platform is a marketplace, not the seller. A completed order is an agreement between you and the other user.
* Meetup cash or DuitNow transfers happen between you and the seller. RacketDiction does not hold that money in escrow.
* There is no guaranteed authenticity check on every racket.
* Ratings and reviews are useful signals. They do not make a transaction safe by themselves.
