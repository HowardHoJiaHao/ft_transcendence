# Dealing Methods & Safe Transactions

On RacketDiction, buyers and sellers negotiate and fulfill transactions directly. We support two primary dealing methods:

## 1. Meet Up (Face-to-Face Transaction)
Highly recommended for badminton equipment, as it allows the buyer to inspect the racket's condition physically before paying.
* **Suggested Locations:** Deal in safe, highly public, and well-lit areas, such as:
  * Local badminton halls/arenas.
  * LRT/MRT stations.
  * Malls or busy cafes.
* **Racket Inspection Tips:**
  * Check the frame for hairline cracks under the grommets.
  * Inspect the shaft for composite cracks.
  * Shake the racket to verify there are no internal carbon graphite noises or loose pieces inside the handle.
* **Payment:** Pay only *after* inspecting the item. Cash or instant DuitNow transfer is standard.

## 2. Mailing & Delivery
Use this if the seller and buyer are in different states (e.g., Kuala Lumpur and Penang).
* **Shipping Fees:** Clarify who covers postage (typically the buyer).
* **Tracking:** Sellers should ship via reliable couriers (J&T Express, PosLaju, DHL Ecommerce) and immediately provide the buyer with a working tracking number.
* **Safety Warning:** Avoid sending direct banking transfers to unverified, unrated sellers. Always check reviews before committing to mailing.
