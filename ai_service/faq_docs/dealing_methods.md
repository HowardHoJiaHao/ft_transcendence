# Dealing Methods and Safe Transactions

On RacketDiction, sellers choose how an item can change hands when they create the listing. Buyers then either arrange that handover in chat or place an order in the cart.

The two listing dealing methods are:

* **Meet Up** — face to face, usually in the seller’s city.
* **Mailing or Delivery** — courier between cities or when a meet-up is impractical.

A listing can offer one or both. Checkout has a separate choice: **Meet up / COD** versus **Stripe sandbox card payment**. Those checkout options sit on top of the listing method; they do not replace inspecting the item.

## 1. Meet up (face to face)

This is the most practical method for used rackets because you can inspect the frame before you pay.

Suggested places:

* Local badminton halls or arenas around peak booking times
* LRT / MRT stations and concourses
* Malls or busy cafes

How to run the meet-up:

1. Agree the exact spot, time, and what you will bring (cash, extra grip, original bag).
2. Arrive in a public, well-lit area. Tell a friend where you are going if the item is expensive.
3. Inspect before paying.
4. Pay with cash or instant DuitNow after you are satisfied.
5. If you used the cart, the seller should later mark the order **delivered** in My Shop.

Racket inspection tips:

* Check the frame for hairline cracks, especially around the grommets and the 12 o’clock / 6 o’clock areas.
* Inspect the shaft for composite cracks or a soft spot.
* Shake the racket and listen for loose graphite or a rattle in the handle.
* Look at the cone, paint, and any serial marking. Compare with photos the seller sent in chat.
* If it is strung, note the tension and any broken grommets.

Pay only after that inspection. Do not leave a deposit “to hold” the racket with someone you have never met.

## 2. Mailing and delivery

Use this when you and the seller are in different states, for example Kuala Lumpur and Penang.

Agree in chat, before anyone pays:

* Who pays postage (often the buyer)
* Which courier (J&T Express, PosLaju, DHL eCommerce, and similar)
* How the racket will be packed (tube or hard case is safer than a thin bag)
* What happens if the frame arrives cracked

After posting, the seller should send a working tracking number the same day.

Safety notes for mailing:

* Instant bank transfers to unverified sellers are hard to reverse.
* Check the listing photos, chat answers, and any reviews on that listing first.
* Prefer a recorded cart order so both sides see the same quantity and price.
* The platform is not a courier and does not insure the parcel.

## 3. How this relates to cart checkout

* **Meet up / COD** in the cart creates a pending order. You still meet or arrange collection. Payment is typically cash or DuitNow between the two of you.
* **Stripe sandbox** is a test card payment for the project’s demo seller only. No real money is charged. It is not a replacement for DuitNow in everyday meet-ups.
* If you only chat and pay in person without checking out, the site may not show an order in **My Purchases**. Update or remove the listing yourself after it is gone.

## 4. Disputes

* Raise the problem in RacketDiction chat first and keep the messages.
* For a cart order, the seller can mark delivered or cancel according to what actually happened.
* The platform may help where it can, but it is not obliged to mediate or refund.
* For fraud or harassment, keep screenshots and use the Contact link in the footer.
